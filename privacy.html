Privacy & your data

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This notice explains how WahalaVerse handles information during its public beta. The beta is guest-only: you can play everything without an account. There is no login, no password, and no cloud profile in this release.

1. Who runs WahalaVerse

Operator: Mercy Moyinoluwa Adepoju, trading as Technoestro Solutions, Nigeria.
Privacy contact: info@technoestro.com

For any question or request about your data, email the address above. We respond to reasonable requests about the limited data described here.

2. Who the beta is for (18+)

WahalaVerse is intended for people aged 18 and over. It contains Nigerian adult social humour. When you first open the site you're asked to confirm you're 18 or older; by continuing you confirm this. If you are under 18, please do not use WahalaVerse. This is a lightweight self-confirmation for the beta, not verified age checking.

3. The short version

  • Playing is account-free — we never ask for a legal name, email, password, phone number, address, date of birth or contacts. When you create a challenge you may type a short display name of your choosing (any nickname) to label it; that's optional, isn't a legal identity, and — along with the answers you pick — becomes part of the shareable link (see section 6).
  • Your game progress and drafts are saved only in your own browser on your own device. We cannot read them.
  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) processes your request to serve the site and keeps standard technical logs.
  • A challenge link you share contains the choices you put into it, so anyone with the link can see them.

4. What stays on your device (browser-local)

As you play, some information is stored in your browser's localStorage on the device you're using. This stays on your device, is not sent to us, and we cannot read it:

  • Recent in-game activity on this device (wv_activity).
  • An in-progress "Create Your Own Wahala" draft (wv_cc_draft).
  • An in-progress "Survive Naija 2.0" game, so you can resume it (wv_sn2_run).
  • Small preferences — e.g. your 18+ acknowledgement and whether you've seen the welcome screen (wv_age_ack, wv_intro_*, wv_pending_name).

5. What our host (Vercel) processes

The site is hosted on Vercel. To deliver the pages, Vercel processes your network request — including your IP address, device/browser user-agent, and the pages requested — and keeps standard access and security logs. This is ordinary technical processing needed to run any website and to protect it from abuse. Vercel acts as our hosting processor under its own data-processing terms.

6. Information inside shared challenge links

When you create and share a "Predict My Wahala" or "Create Your Own Wahala" challenge, the details you entered — such as the display name you chose and the questions and answer choices you selected — are encoded into the link itself. Anyone who has the link can open it and inspect those encoded details. So:

  • Only put into a challenge what you're comfortable sharing with whoever gets the link.
  • Don't use the display-name field for sensitive personal information.
  • Because answers are visible in the link, challenges are for fun and are not suitable for prizes, confidential information, or scoring you can't verify.

7. How long information is kept

  • On your device: browser-local data (section 4) stays until it's cleared — by the game's own controls (for example, finishing, replaying or abandoning a run), or by you clearing your browser's site data. We set no server-side deletion period because we never receive this data.
  • Provider logs: Vercel's standard access/security logs are retained for a short, limited period set by Vercel's platform for our plan (by default on the order of about one hour up to a few days, with longer retention only if specifically enabled). These logs are managed by Vercel under its own terms; we do not individually control their contents or configure a custom period, so we don't state a fixed figure of our own.

8. Deletion and its limits

You can remove device-local data yourself at any time (section 7), or use your browser's private/incognito mode so nothing is retained after you close it. Please note:

  • Vercel's standard logs are kept only briefly and are managed by Vercel under its own terms; we don't control their contents or storage. This is a practical limitation of the hosting provider, not a refusal of your rights — if you have a data request about them, email info@technoestro.com and we'll respond and work with our provider as applicable under the law that applies to you.
  • Once you share a challenge link, we cannot recall or delete copies that other people already have, saved or forwarded — the information is in the link they hold.

9. Accounts and cloud saving (not in this beta)

This beta does not offer accounts. There is no sign-in, no email login, no password, and no cross-device cloud saving — your progress lives only in the browser you played in. Account sign-in, data export and account deletion are planned for a future release and are not available now. We will update this notice before any such feature is enabled.

10. Your choices

  • Play entirely as a guest.
  • Clear your WahalaVerse browser storage at any time (your browser's site-data settings).
  • Use private/incognito mode so nothing is retained on the device after you close it.
  • Email info@technoestro.com with any data question.

11. Changes to this notice

If we change how the beta handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date. Material changes (for example, enabling accounts) will be reflected here before they take effect.
