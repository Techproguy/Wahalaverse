Privacy & your data

This page describes the intended data handling for the WahalaVerse MVP. It is a draft. Before collecting real user registrations, the website operator must add their legal identity, a contact email, retention periods, and a procedure to respond to data requests.

What is collected?

If you choose to sign in: display name and email address. For signed-in users, challenges created and completed game activities (game name, score or simulated outcome, and timestamp). We do not need your phone number, address, contacts, date of birth, or passwords.

What guests can do

You may try games without signing in. Recent guest activity may be stored only in the local storage of your current browser. A guest challenge link includes the name and quiz choices selected by its creator so others can play. Anyone with the link may be able to inspect those encoded choices.

How information is used

To authenticate your account, label challenges, store your activities, and let you see your saved history. Email is not added to a marketing list. Challenge creators' chosen display names and challenge content are visible to people with the link. Account email addresses and individual activity histories are not intentionally made public.

Where information lives

Cloud mode uses Supabase Auth and PostgreSQL with row-level security; browser guest mode uses local storage. The operator must identify the actual hosting regions and processors used before launch.

Your choices

You may play as a guest, sign out, or clear local guest records through browser storage settings. Account deletion/export workflows have not yet been implemented, so public sign-ups should not be enabled before a contact method and request process are available.

Children's data

Account registration in this initial beta is intended for people aged 18 and over. The operator must review age controls and relevant Nigerian privacy requirements before general release.
