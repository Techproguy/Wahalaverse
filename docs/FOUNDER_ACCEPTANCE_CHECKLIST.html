What WahalaVerse is

A lightweight, mobile-first Nigerian entertainment website. Everything is guest-first — anyone can play instantly, no sign-up, no download. It runs with no backend and shares challenges as links (great for WhatsApp). Today it offers four experiences:

Predict My WahalaA friendship quiz — your people guess your answers to 7 funny Nigerian scenarios.
Create Your Own WahalaWrite your own 3–10 questions, share a link, friends try to guess you. Drafts autosave.
Survive NaijaA salary-and-life-decisions mini-game.
Assume Their LifeA causal life-simulation — step into a role, make decisions over a year, live with the consequences. Five lives: Founder, CEO, Billionaire, Political Leadership (core) and Afrobeats (additional).

How to open the build

On a computer:

1. git checkout integration-rehearsal
2. python3 -m http.server 8080
3. Open http://localhost:8080

On a phone (same Wi-Fi): open http://<your-computer-IP>:8080. Gameplay works fully; the native WhatsApp share sheet needs an HTTPS address, so on local it falls back to “copy link”. (For full WhatsApp share testing on a phone, a secure preview link is needed — see the last page.)

C · Assume Their Life — five lives

Open Explore → Assume Their Life. The picker should show all five. Each plays to a “Reckoning” that explains what happened and why. Two contrasting runs are given per core role — tap the buttons in order; the result is the same every time.

Founder Life — Zero to One

Diluted: Cofounder 50/50 → Bootstrap → Persevere → Stay lean → Aggressive VC (3× pref) → Head down → Sell → Diluted to Nothing (a real sale, you get crumbs).
Scaled: Go solo → Bootstrap → Pivot to PMF → Senior hire + ESOP → Clean round → Rally → Sell → Venture Scaled.
Same “sell” action, opposite payout — because of the ownership terms you set earlier.
Founder Life plays to its Reckoning; the two runs end differently and the difference makes sensePASS (auto)

CEO Life — Corner Office

Turnaround: Bet on growth → Retain CFO → Invest in R&D → Negotiate activist → Raise equity → Rally → Deliver → Turnaround Hero.

Release gates (what each release needs)

🟢 Guest-only release (no accounts, no data collected on our servers)

Everything in this review is guest-first. This release does not enable accounts, so Milestone 2 is not a blocker for it.

  • Your acceptance of guest gameplay (sections A–D)
  • Create Your Own Wahala + autosave (section B)
  • All five Assume Their Life roles (section C)
  • Privacy hardening verified (section E)
  • A published Privacy Policy with a genuine working contact + accurate guest-data disclosures covering all three: (1) device-local storage (recent activity + draft in your browser), (2) hosting-provider processing (Vercel handles your requests/IP/logs to serve the site), and (3) personal content inside shared challenge links (the name & answers you enter travel in the URL to anyone you share with). Do not claim guest data stays only on the device.
  • Full regression testing (automated ✅; your spot-checks)
